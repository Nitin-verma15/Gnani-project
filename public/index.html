How it works

One sentence in. A correct ledger entry out.

  1. 1

    You say it

    However you'd tell your helper. Any mix of Hindi and English.

    "Ramesh ko 500 udhaar diya"
  2. 2

    Munshi understands

    It works out who, how much and what kind of entry. If it isn't sure, it asks.

    Customer
    Ramesh
    Sale
    ₹500
    Paid now
    ₹0
  3. 3

    The books update

    Saved straight away and read back to you. Misheard? One tap undoes it.

    Sale ₹500 to Ramesh, ₹500 on udhaar Undo

Under the hood

The AI talks. The ledger does the maths.

  1. Voice inGnani Prisma speech-to-text, primed with your customers' names
  2. AI agentClaude with 18 business tools
  3. LedgerSQLite, exact to the paisa
  4. Voice outGnani Timbre, in a natural Hinglish voice

Figures come from tools

Totals are calculated by the ledger and fetched through tools, and the assistant is told not to quote numbers from memory.

Balances are always calculated

Udhaar is worked out from the ledger each time, so it can't drift out of step.

Refuses to guess

Ambiguous names, future dates and payments from unknown customers go back as a question.

Next on the roadmap

Stock tracking, GST invoices, WhatsApp reminders and more Indian languages, which Gnani's speech models already cover.
