Say what happened at the counter, in English, Hindi or Hinglish. Munshi writes the ledger, keeps every rupee of udhaar, and answers when you ask how business is going.

01Bolo

Say it the way you'd tell your helper.

Mix Hindi and English freely. Names, amounts in words, "kal", "baad mein dega": Munshi understands the counter's language.

02Likh diya

Written into the ledger, exact to the paisa.

Every entry is saved the moment you say it and read back to you. Misheard? One tap undoes it. If a name could be two people, it asks.

03Hisaab

Ask how business is going. Get a straight answer.

"Is hafte kaisa raha?" Sales, spending and who owes what, compared with last week, worked out by the ledger and never guessed.

Munshi keeps every balance, warns when stock runs low, prints bills with GST, and drafts a polite WhatsApp reminder with your UPI ID.

Features

One munshi. The whole shop.

Everything a small business keeps in its head and its registers, kept for you, and never more than a sentence away.

  1. 01

    Udhaar that never gets lost

    Every credit sale and repayment, with balances worked out from the ledger, so they can't drift.

    "Kaun kitna baaki hai?"

  2. 02

    Answers, not charts

    Sales, spending and top customers, compared with the last period, spoken in plain words.

    "Is hafte business kaisa raha?"

  3. 03

    Stock that counts itself

    Sales take items off the shelf, deliveries put them back, and it tells you what's running low.

    "Dal kitni bachi hai?"

  4. 04

    Bills, GST and UPI

    Every sale gets a printable bill, with CGST and SGST worked out and your UPI ID for payment.

    "Pichli sale ka bill dikhao"

  5. 05

    Reminders on WhatsApp

    A polite message, drafted for you. One tap sends it from your own phone. Nothing goes out on its own.

    "Ramesh ko reminder bhejo"

  6. 06

    Safe with your money

    Two customers called Ramesh? It asks which one. Future dates are refused. Every entry can be undone.

    "Galat ho gaya, hata do"

Technology

The AI talks. The ledger does the maths.

  1. 01Voice inGnani Prisma speech-to-text, primed with your customers' names
  2. 02AI agentClaude, with 23 business tools and no guessing
  3. 03LedgerSQLite on your computer, exact to the paisa
  4. 04Voice outGnani Timbre, in a natural Hinglish voice

Figures come from tools

Totals are calculated by the ledger and fetched through tools. The assistant never quotes numbers from memory.

Balances are calculated

Udhaar and stock are worked out from every entry each time, so they can't drift out of step.

Refuses to guess

Ambiguous names, future dates and payments from unknown customers come back as a question.

Your books stay yours

One file on your computer, backed up daily, downloadable as CSV. A PIN can lock it.
